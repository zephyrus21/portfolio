I'm a software engineer working on event-driven backends — the kind that quietly move a hundred million messages a day and aren't allowed to drop any of them.

At Mable.ai I build Go microservices for the ad-tech pipeline: ingest from Meta, Google, Pinterest and Shopify; transform, attribute, persist. The work lives across GCP Pub/Sub, Postgres, ClickHouse and Redis, instrumented with Prometheus and Grafana so failure has a face.

Before that, telemetry at UIDAI and a brief stint in frontend at Omikron. I care about latency budgets, error messages, and treating the next engineer with respect.
